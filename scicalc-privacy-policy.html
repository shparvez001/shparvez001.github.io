Scientific Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Scientific Calculator is a browser extension (Microsoft Edge and Chromium-based browsers) and an installable web app (PWA) that provides a scientific calculator, complex number arithmetic and number base conversion. This policy applies to both and explains what data they handle.

What Scientific Calculator does with your data

  • Nothing leaves your device. Every calculation, complex number operation and base conversion is performed locally in your browser. The numbers and expressions you enter are not sent anywhere.
  • No data is stored. The extension and web app do not save your calculations, history or settings. When you close the calculator, what you typed is gone.

What Scientific Calculator does not do

  • It does not collect, store or transmit your browsing history.
  • It does not collect personal information, and it has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking.
  • It does not make network requests while you use it, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not run any remote code.

Offline use (web app only)

The web app uses a service worker to save its own files (page, styles, scripts and icons) in your browser's cache so it can open without an internet connection. This cache contains only the app itself, never anything you enter. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

When you first load the web app, the server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (for example, IP address and time of request), as most web servers do. This is controlled by the hosting provider and is not collected or used by the app itself. The browser extension has no server and generates no such logs.

Permissions

  • Extension: requests no special permissions. It cannot read or change webpages, tabs, history or any browser data. It only displays its calculator popup when you click the toolbar icon.
  • Web app: requests no permissions (no location, camera, microphone, notifications or contacts).

Children

Scientific Calculator does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
