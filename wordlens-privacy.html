WordLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

WordLens is a browser extension that shows a word's definition when you double-click it on a webpage. This policy explains what data it handles.

What WordLens does with your data

  • The word you double-click. When you double-click a word, only that single word is sent to the dictionary service at api.dictionaryapi.dev to fetch its definition. Nothing else from the page is sent: no surrounding text, page address, page content or account details.
  • Your on/off setting. WordLens stores one preference (whether the extension is on or off) using the browser's extension storage. If you are signed in to your browser with sync enabled, the browser may sync this setting across your devices.

What WordLens does not do

  • It does not collect, store or transmit your browsing history.
  • It does not collect personal information, and it has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties for any purpose other than the dictionary lookup described above.
  • It does not run any remote code.

Third-party service

Definitions are provided by the Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev). Requests to that service are subject to its own terms and privacy practices. Links shown in the definition panel to Merriam-Webster and Google open those sites in a new tab only if you click them.

Permissions

  • Storage: to remember your on/off setting.
  • Access to api.dictionaryapi.dev: to look up the word you double-click.
  • Running on webpages: to detect a double-click on a word and show the definition panel.

Children

WordLens does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
